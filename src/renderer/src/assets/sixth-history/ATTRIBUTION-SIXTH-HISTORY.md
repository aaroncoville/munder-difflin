The `occult` app theme is Sixth History content. It is built under Weather
Factory Ltd's Sixth History Community Licence, which is what makes it legal to
dress this app in the idiom of the Secret Histories at all.

- **Licence:** <https://weatherfactory.biz/sixth-history-community-licence/>
- **Licence last updated:** 28 November 2022
- **Read and assets downloaded:** 27 August 2026
- **Logo file:** `logo.png`, the 300×300 PNG published on the licence page at
  `https://weatherfactory.biz/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/sixth-history-logo-300x300.png`

## The obligations, and where each one is discharged

1. **Say it is unofficial.** The licence's own suggested wording is rendered by
   `components/SixthHistoryCredit.tsx`, which mounts in the Settings footer
   whenever the occult theme is active. The sentence it renders, verbatim from
   the licence page:

   > "[Product] is unofficial content based on [the IP] by Weather Factory Ltd.
   > You can find out more and support [the IP] at [www.weatherfactory.biz]."

2. **Display the Sixth History logo** on any content created under the licence.
   Recolouring and resizing are permitted; substantial alteration is not. The
   credit component renders `logo.png` beside the wording. The logo ships as
   published and is only inverted in CSS, because the mark is black on
   transparent and the occult theme's grounds are near-black — that is the
   permitted recolour, not an alteration of the mark.

3. **Use the IP as part of your own creation, not on its own.** The theme is a
   dress for an existing application, not a redistribution of Weather Factory
   material.

4. **Fair Use of the writing only.** No Secret Histories prose is copied. Any
   text written in that register elsewhere in this project is original writing,
   not quotation.

5. **Revenue cap of £50,000 per annum** across all content made under the
   licence. Noted; this project is free and open source.

6. **Only the art assets the licence page offers.** The single asset taken is
   the Sixth History logo above. No Cultist Simulator, BOOK OF HOURS or Lady
   Afterwards logo is used, and no other Weather Factory artwork.

   This obligation has one live surface: `scene/study/assets/portraits`, the
   drop-in pack the Study puts on assistants' cards. Because it is a directory
   anyone can add a file to, the rule is enforced at the directory rather than
   at review time — only two provenances are admissible there:

   - an image from the community asset pack the licence page publishes, or
   - artwork generated for this project.

   Every file's provenance is recorded in the table below as it lands. A
   portrait with no row here has not been cleared. The pack ships empty, and an
   empty pack is a supported state: cards fall back to a monogram.

   | Portrait file | Provenance |
   |---|---|
   | _(none yet)_ | — |

7. **Comply with local law.** Noted.

## What is NOT covered

The licence explicitly excludes the *Cultist Simulator*, *BOOK OF HOURS* and
*The Lady Afterwards* logos, and any content from Weather Factory's other
products. None appear here.
